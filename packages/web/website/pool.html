Getting Started

New to mining? Follow these steps

1

Get a Wallet

Download the RetardioChain wallet from our releases page or use the Web Flasher to set up a hardware wallet.

2

Configure Miner

Configure your mining software to connect to our pool. See our setup guide for detailed instructions.

3

Configure and Mine

Use the mining commands above, replacing YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS with your actual RetardioChain address (starts with R).

4

Track Your Earnings

Use the stats lookup above to monitor your hashrate and pending balance. Payouts are processed automatically.
