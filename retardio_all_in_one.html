2. Go to Settings → Pool
3. Pool URL: stratum+tcp://POOL_HOST_HERE:3333
4. Worker: Your wallet address
5. Password: x
6. Save and apply

How Solo Pool Mining Works

Solo Pool = Best of Both Worlds

Unlike traditional pools where rewards are split, our solo pool lets you keep 100% of every block you find. The pool handles all the heavy lifting (running the node, validating blocks, broadcasting to the network) - you just point your miner and start hashing.
